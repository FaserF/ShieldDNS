Detailed Transparency Disclosures (GDPR Articles 13 & 14)

1. Web Visit (HTTP / HTTPS)

When accessing this information page or the web dashboard, the web server temporarily processes technical connection data (IP address, browser User-Agent, request timestamp) solely to deliver requested assets. No third-party tracking scripts, analytics cookies, or advertising tags are utilized.

2. DNS Resolution & Protocol Tunneling (DoH / DoT / DoQ / Plain)

When your device queries this DNS resolver, requested domain names are evaluated locally to detect malware, phishing, and unwanted tracking domains. Once resolved, responses are returned directly to your device without external profiling.

3. Data Retention Period

Resolved queries stored for dashboard visibility are purged automatically and irrevocably after the configured retention period of {{if eq .RetentionDays 0}}0 days (logging disabled){{else}}{{.RetentionDays}} days{{end}}.

4. Zero Third-Party Sharing

DNS records, client IP mappings, and user query histories are strictly confidential and are never sold, leased, or transmitted to commercial advertising networks, data aggregators, or telemetry platforms.
